WEBLOGIC - REGENERATE DEMO PKCS12 CERTIFICATES ON WINDOWS
===========================================================

BACKGROUND
-----------
DemoIdentity.p12 and DemoTrust.p12 were copied from another machine.
They are expired and have the wrong hostname. Steps below regenerate
them in the same location with the correct hostname.


STEP 1 - NOTE YOUR EXACT FILE PATH
-------------------------------------
From your dir command, the path should look like:

  C:\Oracle\Middleware\wlserver\server\lib\DemoIdentity.p12
  C:\Oracle\Middleware\wlserver\server\lib\DemoTrust.p12

Use YOUR actual path in all commands below.


STEP 2 - DELETE BOTH OLD FILES
--------------------------------
  del C:\Oracle\Middleware\wlserver\server\lib\DemoIdentity.p12
  del C:\Oracle\Middleware\wlserver\server\lib\DemoTrust.p12


STEP 3 - REGENERATE DemoIdentity.p12
--------------------------------------
  keytool -genkeypair ^
    -alias DemoIdentity ^
    -keyalg RSA ^
    -keysize 2048 ^
    -validity 365 ^
    -storetype PKCS12 ^
    -keystore C:\Oracle\Middleware\wlserver\server\lib\DemoIdentity.p12 ^
    -storepass DemoIdentityKeyStorePassPhrase ^
    -keypass DemoIdentityKeyStorePassPhrase ^
    -dname "CN=%COMPUTERNAME%, OU=Demo, O=Demo, L=Demo, ST=Demo, C=US"


STEP 4 - EXPORT CERT AND RECREATE DemoTrust.p12
-------------------------------------------------
Export the cert from DemoIdentity.p12:

  keytool -exportcert ^
    -alias DemoIdentity ^
    -keystore C:\Oracle\Middleware\wlserver\server\lib\DemoIdentity.p12 ^
    -storetype PKCS12 ^
    -storepass DemoIdentityKeyStorePassPhrase ^
    -file C:\Oracle\Middleware\wlserver\server\lib\DemoCert.crt

Import into DemoTrust.p12:

  keytool -importcert ^
    -alias DemoTrust ^
    -file C:\Oracle\Middleware\wlserver\server\lib\DemoCert.crt ^
    -keystore C:\Oracle\Middleware\wlserver\server\lib\DemoTrust.p12 ^
    -storetype PKCS12 ^
    -storepass DemoTrustKeyStorePassPhrase ^
    -noprompt


STEP 5 - VERIFY THE NEW CERT
------------------------------
  keytool -list -v ^
    -keystore C:\Oracle\Middleware\wlserver\server\lib\DemoIdentity.p12 ^
    -storetype PKCS12 ^
    -storepass DemoIdentityKeyStorePassPhrase

Check that:
  - Owner CN shows your current machine name
  - Valid from shows today's date
  - Valid until shows 1 year from now


STEP 6 - RESTART WEBLOGIC
---------------------------
  %DOMAIN_HOME%\bin\stopWebLogic.cmd
  %DOMAIN_HOME%\bin\startWebLogic.cmd


===========================================================
NOTES:
- Replace C:\Oracle\Middleware\wlserver\server\lib\ with
  your actual path from the dir command output.

- If keytool is not recognized, run this first:
    set PATH=%JAVA_HOME%\bin;%PATH%

- Default demo passphrases used (standard WebLogic):
    Identity : DemoIdentityKeyStorePassPhrase
    Trust    : DemoTrustKeyStorePassPhrase

- These are self-signed/demo certs for DEV/TEST only.
  Do NOT use in production.
===========================================================

**Idea Name**  
AI-Powered Bill Forecast using Unbilled Billable Charges (No Bill Segment / FT Generation)

**Problem Statement**
- Customers and internal teams need visibility into the upcoming bill amount *before* the original bill is generated across monthly, bi-weekly, weekly, and daily frequencies.
- Standard Trial Billing creates actual Bill Segments and Financial Transactions, which is undesirable due to performance, audit, data volume, and process constraints.
- Current process lacks a lightweight, non-intrusive way to preview the bill purely from existing billable charges.
- There is no intelligent layer to detect anomalies, project incomplete periods, or explain variances in the forecast.

**High Level Solution**
- Build a custom Business Service / Algorithm that identifies and aggregates only eligible unbilled Billable Charges based on the same eligibility rules used by C1-BLGEN (dates, recurring flag, frequency, Bill After, adhoc/regular, currency conversion).
- Expose the forecast via a Zone / UI Map on Account 360 or Control Central showing summary total + detailed line items without ever creating Bill Segments or FTs.
- Support all four billing frequencies by deriving the correct cutoff/period from the account’s bill cycle schedule.
- Integrate AI/ML for anomaly detection on forecast amounts/volumes, mid-cycle usage projection (especially useful for daily/weekly), and natural-language explanation of the forecast vs historical bills.
- Optionally pre-rate SQI-based charges or call existing pricing simulation services so rate-based amounts are also accurate without full bill generation.

**Benefits**
- Provides accurate, real-time forecast visibility without generating any Bill Segments or Financial Transactions, keeping the system clean.
- Improves bill quality and reduces post-bill corrections by enabling early review and anomaly detection.
- Works uniformly across monthly, bi-weekly, weekly, and daily billing cycles.
- Leverages AI for smarter insights (anomaly detection, projections, explanations), increasing user confidence and operational efficiency.
